What Neck Pain Feels Like

Can't turn to check
your blind spot.

Neck pain can feel like a stiff, locked neck you can’t turn to check your blind spot, an ache that spreads into the shoulder blade, tingling or weakness running down the arm (a pinched nerve), or a headache that starts at the base of the skull. Desk work and phones leave many people with a daily tension that never fully lets go.

The causes we treat most in Ravenna are mechanical and postural neck pain, cervical disc and nerve-root irritation (pinched nerve), whiplash after a car accident, and cervicogenic headaches driven by the upper neck joints and muscles.

Most neck pain and the headaches tied to it respond quickly to hands-on care and targeted exercise. Ohio is a direct-access state, so you can start physical therapy at Cowboy Healthcare in Ravenna without a doctor's referral, and you can pay cash if you prefer.

Common Questions

What patients in Ravenna
always ask

Do I need a referral for neck physical therapy?

No. Ohio direct access means you can book a Doctor of Physical Therapy at Cowboy Healthcare in Ravenna without a referral. If your insurance requires one, we’ll let you know upfront.

Can I pay cash for neck treatment?

Yes. Flat-rate cash-pay and drop-in neck visits are available with no pre-authorization, and HSA/FSA cards are accepted.

Can physical therapy help my headaches?

Often, yes. Headaches that start at the base of the skull (cervicogenic headaches) frequently come from the upper neck, and treating those joints and muscles can meaningfully reduce how often and how hard they hit.

Is my arm tingling a pinched nerve?

It may be. Tingling, pain or weakness down the arm often points to a nerve root in the neck, which usually responds well to physical therapy. We’ll assess it and refer on if anything needs further workup.
