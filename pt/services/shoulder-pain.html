Shoulder Conditions We Treat

Named, and treated
at the source

Rotator Cuff Injuries

Tears, strains, and tendinopathy of the rotator cuff — the most common cause of shoulder pain and weakness, including after a fall or overuse.

Shoulder Impingement

Pinching of the rotator cuff tendons under the shoulder blade, causing pain when you lift or reach overhead.

Frozen Shoulder

Adhesive capsulitis — progressive stiffness and loss of motion. Guided, staged therapy restores range safely.

Shoulder Bursitis & Tendinitis

Inflammation of the bursa or tendons that makes everyday movement painful.

Labral Tears & Instability

Rehabilitation for SLAP and labral tears, dislocations, and a shoulder that feels loose or gives way.

Post-Surgical Shoulder

Structured recovery after rotator cuff repair, labral repair, or shoulder replacement — coordinated with your surgeon.
